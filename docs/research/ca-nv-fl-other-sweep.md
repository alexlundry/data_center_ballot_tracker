# Research notes: California, Nevada, Florida and other-state sweep

Compiled 2026-10-15 from web search summaries (direct page fetches were blocked in the build environment). Verbatim ballot text is marked where it was seen; everything else is a summary from reporting.

## November 3, 2026 measures

### Alhambra, CA — Measure DB (Los Angeles County)
- Council-referred ordinance measure (Ordinance O2M26-4868, adopted July 27, 2026).
- Question (LA County Registrar list rev. 8-14-2026): "Shall an ordinance be adopted affirming the Council adopted ban on 'Data Centers' by amending the Alhambra Municipal Code to again define 'Data Centers' and prohibit them in all zones of the City?" Simple majority.
- YES = ban (restrict). Locks in the council's existing zoning ban so only voters can repeal it.
- Links: https://www.alhambraca.gov/175/Ballot-Measures · https://alhambraca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/8289/FAQs-About-Measure-HT-and-DB · https://content.lavote.gov/docs/rrcc/documents/measures-appearing-on-the-ballot---november-3-2026-rev-8-14-2026-v-4.pdf · https://laist.com/news/alhambra-ballot-measure-data-center-ban-2026
- Results: LA County RR/CC https://results.lavote.gov (in-house ENR; text pages at /text-results/{electionId}; JSON downloads at https://www.lavote.gov/home/voting-elections/current-elections/election-results-file-downloads). CA SOS does not report local measures.
- Lat/long 34.0953, -118.1270.

### Boulder City, NV — Ballot Question 1 (Clark County)
- Council-referred land-use authorization required by City Charter §144(1); referred 3-1 on Feb 24, 2026. A separate citizen initiative failed to qualify.
- Question (city Explanation & Digest, near-verbatim): "Shall data center facilities be an approved land use within the Eldorado Valley Transfer Area outside of the Multi-species Habitat Conservation Easement?"
- YES = allow (pro-data-center). Makes roughly 14,000 acres of city-owned desert eligible for data center leases; projects still need the Land Management Plan process and council approval.
- Links: https://www.bcnv.org/DocumentCenter/View/16416/Ballot-Question-1-Explanation-and-Digest · https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/09/11/ballot-measure-gives-boulder-city-voters-a-chance-to-weigh-in-on-data-centers/ · https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/environment/data-centers-will-be-on-the-ballot-in-this-southern-nevada-city-3727498/
- Results: Clark County ENR https://elections.clarkcountynv.gov/ElectionResultsTV/ENR.aspx ; NV SOS county pages carry city questions (expected https://www.nvsos.gov/SOSelectionPages/results/2026StateWideGeneral/Clark.aspx and a silverstate2026gen site). Machine-readable feeds unconfirmed.
- Lat/long 35.9786, -114.8325.

### Edgewater, FL — Charter Amendment 4 (Volusia County)
- Council-referred charter amendment; final reading Aug 3, 2026. Council also passed an ordinance ban Sept 14, 2026 as a bridge.
- Official title/summary not retrieved; reported substance: prohibits permitting, construction, expansion or operation of new data centers in city limits (cloud, AI, crypto mining, colocation).
- YES = ban (restrict).
- Links: https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/08/04/edgewater-voters-could-decide-whether-to-ban-certain-data-centers-in-november/ · https://www.wftv.com/news/local/edgewater-residents-vote-potential-ai-data-center-bans-this-november/HR2N7N26OFC2JP56IMGIQ6TABA/
- Results: Volusia SOE https://www.volusiaelections.gov/elections/election-results/2026-election-results/november-3-2026-general-election.stml ; VR Systems ENR https://vrcdn.electionsfl.org/StaticPages/volusia.html. FL Division of Elections does not report municipal questions.
- Lat/long 28.9889, -80.9023.

### East Whiteland Township, PA — board expansion referendum (Chester County) — EXCLUDED from the tracker (decision Oct 15: not a data center question on its face)
- Citizen-initiated question to elect two additional supervisors (3 to 5), born of opposition to a proposed ~1.5M sq ft data center on a former Superfund site. Not a direct restriction; proponents are anti-data-center.
- Links: https://chesco.org/DocumentCenter/View/85854/CHESTER_2026-GENERAL_OFFICIAL-LIST-OF-REFERENDUMS · https://www.inquirer.com/news/pennsylvania/east-whiteland-board-supervisors-data-center-20260819.html

- Lat/long 40.045, -75.545.

## Decided earlier in 2026
- Monterey Park, CA — Measure NDC (June 2, 2026): General Plan amendment prohibiting data centers citywide. PASSED 10,321 yes (88.34%) to 1,362 no (secondary sources citing certified LA County results). Lat/long 34.0625, -118.1228.
- Georgia May 19, 2026 party primary advisory questions (non-binding, county party committees): Bulloch (GOP Q9 and Dem Q3; ~76% R and ~78% D voted NO on allowing data centers, per a commissioner's report), Glynn, DeKalb, Columbia, Paulding (results not retrieved; county Clarity results have them).

## Removed, blocked or pending (not on Nov 3)
- Frederick County, MD overlay-zone referendum: blocked by the Maryland Supreme Court (order June 30, 2026; opinion July 28, 2026). Zoning ordinances not subject to referendum.
- Weld County, CO: two POWER petitions (referendum on Global AI approval; initiative requiring voter approval of data centers). If certified, charter requires a special election within 60 days. Not on Nov 3.
- La Pine, OR: citizen initiative reportedly missed the signature deadline (Bend Bulletin, Aug 14).
- Statewide: none anywhere in 2026. Ohio Conserve Ohio initiative re-targeted 2027; Massachusetts indirect initiative targets Nov 2028.

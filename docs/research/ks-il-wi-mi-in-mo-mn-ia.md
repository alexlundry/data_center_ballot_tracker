# Research notes: Kansas, Illinois, Wisconsin, Michigan (plus IN, MO, MN, IA sweep)

Compiled 2026-10-15 from web search summaries. Nov 3 measures: Kansas 3, Illinois 7, Wisconsin 1. Decided earlier: Port Washington WI (Apr 7), Augusta Township MI (Aug 4), Wrightstown WI (Aug 11). Indiana, Missouri, Minnesota, Iowa: no ballot measures found.

## Kansas (all citizen-initiated ordinances under K.S.A. 12-3013; YES = ban)
- **Emporia, Lyon County — Question #2** (companion Question #3 bans Tier 2/3 battery storage). "Shall the following ordinance be adopted?" Ordinance prohibits construction, installation, operation and maintenance of High Impact Data Centers in all zoning districts; HIDC = peak electrical load over 10 MW or daily water draw over 100,000 gallons, incl. server farms, colocation, AI training, crypto mining. Court ordered it onto the ballot; city's declaratory-judgment action on whether it is a proper initiative subject is still pending (if approved, takes effect only after a ruling). Supporters: Emporia Neighbors United. Opponents: Regional Development Association of East Central Kansas. Links: https://www.emporiaks.gov/1600/2026-City-of-Emporia-Ballot-Information · https://www.ksnt.com/news/local-news/court-orders-data-center-question-to-be-put-on-nov-ballot-in-emporia/ · https://kansasreflector.com/2026/09/13/who-bankrolls-emporias-data-center-bid-is-the-3-million-question-the-answer-is-sought-in-court/. Results: Lyon County Clerk https://lyoncountyks.gov (ES&S PDF summary reports; 2026 primary pattern /391/2026-Primary-Election-Results). 38.4039, -96.1817.
- **El Dorado, Butler County** — citizen-initiated ordinance (Protect the Heartland) prohibiting High Impact Data Centers and Tier 2/3 BESS; cannot be amended or repealed by the commission for ten years except by voters. District court ruled petition legislative; commission placed it 5-0 (Aug 25). Links: https://www.eldoks.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/688 · https://www.ksn.com/news/el-dorado-commission-votes-to-put-data-center-ban-on-ballot-in-november/ · https://www.kwch.com/2026/08/27/el-dorado-voters-have-say-potential-future-data-centers/. Results: Butler County Clerk/Election Office (bucoks.com, unverified). 37.8172, -96.8622.
- **Ottawa, Franklin County** — citizen-initiated ordinance (Ottawa United) prohibiting data centers using more than 25 MW. Judge Eric Godderz ordered it onto the ballot; hearing Oct 20, 2026 on whether the ordinance is legislative or administrative. Targeted Lightfield Energy "Proximity Park" (contract since terminated). Links: https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/data-centers/franklin-county-judge-rules-data-center-question-will-go-on-november-ballot · https://kofo.com/2026/09/06/court-order-data-center-issue-to-the-ballot/. Results: Franklin County Clerk (franklincoks.org, unverified). 38.6156, -95.2678.
- Kansas SOS ENT (https://ent.sos.ks.gov/) does not carry city questions.

## Illinois (all advisory, non-binding)
- **City of Bloomington Township (coterminous with the city), McLean County** — "Should the City of Bloomington implement a moratorium on the creation of any large-scale data centers?" Placed by electors at a June 1 special town meeting. YES = moratorium. Links: https://bloomingtonelectionsil.gov/advisory_datacenter_moratorium_question/ · https://www.wglt.org/local-news/2026-06-02/bloomington-residents-put-questions-on-data-centers-grocery-tax-international-weapons-to-nov-vote · https://www.25newsnow.com/2026/10/14/bloomington-voters-will-see-an-advisory-question-data-centers-november-ballot/. Results: Bloomington Election Commission https://www.becvote.org (NOT the county clerk). 40.4842, -88.9937.
- **Joliet Township, Will County — "Data Center Construction"** — "Should the Joliet City Council approve construction of AI data centers?" Placed by the township board after the council's 8-1 approval of a 795-acre campus. YES = allow. Links: https://www.willcountyclerk.gov/november-3-2026-general-election/referenda-list-november-3-2026-general-election/ · https://patch.com/illinois/joliet/joliet-2026-voter-guide-everything-you-need-know. Results: Will County Clerk https://www.willcountyclerk.gov. 41.5250, -88.0817.
- **Chouteau, Granite City, Nameoki and Venice Townships, Madison County** — "Shall the construction and operation of new data centers be permitted in ___ Township?" YES = allow. Links: https://www.thetelegraph.com/projects/2026/november-voter-guide-alton-illinois/. Results: Madison County Clerk https://www.madisoncountyil.gov/departments/county_clerk/elections/election_results.php. Coordinates: Granite City 38.70,-90.15; Nameoki 38.72,-90.10; Venice 38.67,-90.17; Chouteau 38.75,-90.07.
- **Morgan County (county-wide)** — "Shall there be restrictions imposed on the construction and operation of data centers in Morgan County?" Citizen petition (1,100+ signatures). YES = restrict. Links: https://www.myjournalcourier.com/news/article/morgan-county-data-center-referendum-22340222.php · https://wlds.com/nov-ref-on-data-centers/. Results: Morgan County Clerk (URL unverified). 39.72, -90.20.
- Illinois State Board of Elections does not report local referenda; 108 local election authorities do.

## Wisconsin
- **Janesville, Rock County (Nov 3)** — direct legislation (Wis. Stat. 9.20): approval of development agreements, projects, sales and leases at the GM/JATCO sites involving more than $450 million in project costs must be decided by a majority of electors. Triggered by Viridian Partners' proposed 800 MW campus. YES = require voter approval (restrictive). Links: https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/02/12/janesville-wisconsin-voters-to-decide-nov-3-2026-initiative-requiring-approval-for-development-projects-exceeding-450-million-at-gm-jatco-site-such-as-proposed-data-center/ · https://www.wmtv15news.com/2026/08/24/janesville-referendum-decide-vote-requirement-gmjatco-site-projects/. Results: Rock County Clerk static HTML, expected https://elections.co.rock.wi.us/20261103.html (pattern from prior elections). 42.6828, -89.0187.
- **Port Washington, Ozaukee County (Apr 7, DECIDED, PASSED)** — direct legislation barring the city from creating or approving a TID with base value or project costs of $10M or more without voter approval. YES 2,710 (66.4%) – NO 1,371. Lawsuit by MMAC et al. pending. Links: https://www.ozaukeepress.com/content/port-tif-referendum-approved-landslide · https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/04/port-washington-data-center-project-voters-referendum-tif-district-wisconsin/. 43.3872, -87.8756.
- **Wrightstown, Brown County (Aug 11, DECIDED, advisory FAILED)** — whether the village should support municipal utility infrastructure tied to a large-scale data center. YES 151 (12.8%) – NO 1,033 (87.2%). Links: https://www.wbay.com/2026/08/12/wrightstown-residents-vote-no-data-center-referendum/. 44.3261, -88.1762.
- WEC publishes no election-night results; county clerks post within ~4 hours of poll close. Directory: https://elections.wi.gov/wisconsin-county-election-websites

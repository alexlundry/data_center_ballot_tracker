# Research notes: Ohio (19 measures in 16 jurisdictions on Nov 3, 2026)

Compiled 2026-10-15 from web search summaries. Official issue numbers are only confirmed for Conneaut (10), Trenton (17) and Grove City (28); the Ohio SOS "November 3, 2026 General Election – Local Questions and Issues (XLSX)" linked from https://www.ohiosos.gov/elections resolves the rest. Nine measures use the Conserve Ohio template (charter amendment banning data centers with aggregate monthly demand or peak load over 25 MW): Conneaut, Defiance, Granville, Oregon, Pataskala, Sunbury, Trenton, Upper Sandusky, plus Piqua (now Feb 2027). Urbana uses a 7.5 MW variant.

Direction: YES = anti-data-center in 13 (Hubbard, Urbana, Sunbury, Conneaut, Defiance, Trenton, Pataskala, Granville, Oregon, Upper Sandusky, Grove City, Sprigg, Monroe). YES = pro-data-center in 6 (Ashville, Wilmington x4, Monroeville advisory).

## Measures

1. **Hubbard (city), Trumbull County** — citizen-initiated ordinance (750+ signatures vs 370). Trumbull BOE list: "Hubbard City – Proposed Ordinance – to approve or reject a ban on data centers within the City limits." Full ban, no MW threshold (the only outright ban in Ohio this November). Targets "Project Milo" near Deer Creek Golf Course; council moratorium Ord. 12-26. YES = ban. Links: https://cityofhubbard-oh.gov/data-center-petitions/ · https://www.tribtoday.com/news/local-news/2026/10/hubbard-voters-to-decide-on-ban-on-data-centers/ · https://www.wfmj.com/news/local-news/hubbard/decision-2026-hubbard-data-center-ban/article_5c7140c4-1faf-45d1-a0e4-3c5593db7d37.html · BOE running list https://boe.co.trumbull.oh.gov/pdfs/Questions%20and%20Issues%20Running%20List%2011-3-26.pdf. 41.1565, -80.5690.
2. **Urbana (city), Champaign County** — citizen-initiated charter amendment (Section 8.07, Article VIII) prohibiting data centers; exemption for single-organization facilities under 7.5 MW peak demand. Thor Equities proposal; federal lawsuit; mayoral recall on same ballot. YES = ban. Links: https://www.urbanacitizen.com/2026/08/31/data-center-ban-will-be-on-urbana-ballot/ · https://www.springfieldnewssun.com/local/urbana-council-puts-data-center-ban-on-november-ballot/article_92d259c3-44d6-5740-aef7-74037cabf026.html. 40.1084, -83.7524.
3. **Sunbury (city), Delaware County** — citizen-initiated charter amendment (Big Walnut for Responsible Growth). Near-official text: "Shall Article XI, Section 11.05 of the Charter of the City of Sunbury be adopted…" prohibiting data centers over 25 MW; self-executing. Targets ~$2B Amazon Data Services campus; moratorium through Jan 31, 2027. YES = ban. Links: https://www.delgazette.com/2026/10/06/data-centers-on-ballot-in-sunbury/ · https://www.nbc4i.com/news/your-local-election-hq/sunbury-residents-to-vote-on-data-center-ban · BOE https://vote.delawarecountyohio.gov/. 40.2423, -82.8591.
4. **Conneaut (city), Ashtabula County — Issue 10** — citizen-initiated charter amendment, 25 MW. No formal proposal (M2 Development feasibility study at Conneaut East Industrial Park). YES = restrict. Links: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/conneaut-charter-amendment-restrict-data-035900617.html · https://www.farmanddairy.com/news/is-a-data-center-coming-to-conneaut-ohio-some-residents-think-so-and-they-want-to-stop-it/929515.html. 41.9478, -80.5545.
5. **Defiance (city), Defiance County** — citizen-initiated charter amendment adding Section 1.12, 25 MW. Council also adopted a 6-month moratorium. YES = restrict. Links: https://www.crescent-news.com/news/local_news/defiance-voters-to-decide-charter-amendment-on-data-center-question/article_ecf536e7-080d-481b-88bd-37b5cddf6034.html. 41.2845, -84.3558.
6. **Trenton (city), Butler County — Issue 17** — citizen-initiated charter amendment (Article IX, Section 9.06): "(A) data center construction is prohibited… aggregate monthly demand or peak load greater than twenty-five megawatts, from any energy source or combination… (D) self-executing." Ohio Supreme Court ordered it to the ballot (State ex rel. Blankenship v. Trenton City Council, 2026-Ohio-3406). AWS campus under construction unaffected; proposed ~600-acre Amazon campus would be blocked. YES = restrict. Links: https://www.wvxu.org/local-news/2026-10-09/data-centers-are-on-the-ballot-in-trenton-heres-what-to-know-about-issue-17 · https://courtnewsohio.gov/cases/2026/SCO/0901/261035.asp · https://www.journal-news.com/local/elections/trenton-voters-will-decide-on-a-data-center-ban-election-2026/article_b2162dd4-ef96-56f3-9d38-c623021b2ee6.html. 39.4809, -84.4577.
7. **Grove City (city), Franklin County — Issue 28 "Community Consent Amendment"** — citizen-initiated charter amendment requiring a public vote on any development of 50+ acres, over 20 MW peak, or over 500,000 gal/day water; developer pays election costs. Grew out of a ~300-acre data center proposal. YES = adds referendum requirement. Links: full text https://www.vote.franklincountyohio.gov/getmedia/dcefafb8-6759-4607-967d-b7154507569a/Grove-City-Charter-Amendment-5 · certified list https://vote.franklincountyohio.gov/getmedia/4001b052-1b90-4f73-b030-2d981e6ec8fa/2026-General-Certified-Questions-and-Issues · https://www.grovecityohio.gov/750/7541/Issue-28-Community-Consent-Amendment-Inf · https://www.nbc4i.com/news/your-local-election-hq/what-is-issue-28-on-grove-citys-november-2026-ballot/. 39.8815, -83.0930.

11. **Upper Sandusky (city), Wyandot County** — citizen-initiated charter amendment, 25 MW (weakest-confirmed; secondary sources only). Opposition to a ~150 MW self-powered facility north of town. YES = ban. BOE https://www.boe.ohio.gov/wyandot/. 40.8273, -83.2813.

## Highest value
1. **FETCHED** (`ohio-sos-2026-11-general-qandi.xlsx`, Sept 18 version) — Ohio Secretary of State, "November 3, 2026 General Election – Local Questions and Issues (XLSX)", linked from https://www.ohiosos.gov/elections. Confirmed every Ohio measure except Conneaut and Sunbury (both absent from the file; Oct 23 recheck corrected the earlier "only Conneaut"); carries no issue numbers and no ballot text beyond a one-line purpose ("Issues Summary" sheet, columns County through Overlap 5). Predates Piqua's Oct 6 move to a Feb 2027 special election. Oct 23 cross-check of the purpose lines against `data/races.csv`: section and ordinance numbers agree for Trenton (9.06), Defiance (1.12), Granville (10.12), Pataskala (11.09), Oregon (Art. XIII Sec. 8), Upper Sandusky (17.13), Perry (133.01) and Wilmington (O-26-04 to O-26-07; the file prints 0-26-07 with a zero). Urbana differs: the file says "Article VII, Section 8.07", our sample-ballot text says Article VIII; 8.07 belongs in Article VIII, so the file is taken as the typo. Monroeville's purpose line reads like an ordinance ("Shall the proposed ordinance allowing data center development be adopted?"); the sample ballot prints an advisory question, which stands. Grove City's line is only "Charter Amendment: XI". A second copy downloaded Oct 23 (`2026-11-general-qandi.xlsx`) is byte-identical.
